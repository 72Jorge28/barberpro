## Placeholders
This template may include placeholder text and UI-only placeholder media.
Buyers are expected to replace placeholders with their own content and brand assets.

## What we DO NOT include
- No copyrighted brand logos
- No trademarked brand names in content
- No paid stock photos by default

## Buyer responsibility
If you add third-party assets (photos, icons, fonts), you must:
1) Verify license terms
2) Keep attribution (if required)
3) Ensure commercial use is allowed

## Recommended safe sources (buyer can use)
- Self-made photos / graphics
- Public domain or properly licensed assets
- Assets with clear commercial licenses
